The free stack

⚡ Render Free

512 MB RAM and 0.1 CPU, 750 instance hours per month, HTTPS and a public *.onrender.com URL. No card required for Free web services. Spins down after 15 idle minutes — the keep-alive handles it.

🐘 Neon Free Postgres

Free forever, no card: 1 GB storage and 100 compute-hours per month, scaling to zero when idle. Your OpenList settings survive redeploys, restarts and spin-downs.

🛠️ Custom builds

Point CUSTOM_BINARY_URL at your fork's release, or CUSTOM_DIST_URL at a custom UI build. ListCloud swaps them in at boot, so you are not limited to the official release.

What "free" actually means

Render: 512 MB RAM, 0.1 CPU, 750 instance hours/month, 15-minute idle spin-down, ephemeral disk, limited free outbound bandwidth. Neon: 1 GB storage and 100 compute-hours/month; the database pauses when the quota is exhausted. Hugging Face Docker Spaces and Koyeb are no longer free without a card, which is why they are not offered here. Free tiers are fine for personal use, not for production SLAs.
